# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Teams use Omada to organize work in shared workspaces and collaborate on boards, cards, documents, comments, and realtime activity.

## Product Purpose

Omada provides a focused collaborative workspace for planning work, managing tasks, and keeping teams synchronized in realtime.

## Brand Personality

Calm, focused, collaborative. The interface should feel dependable and easy to understand while teams are working together.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy, overly animated SaaS interfaces and decorative motion that competes with the user's task.

## Design Principles

- Keep the current task visually clear.
- Make collaboration state and permissions understandable.
- Prefer familiar productivity patterns over novel controls.
- Use motion to communicate state changes, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA, with keyboard-accessible controls and reduced-motion support.
